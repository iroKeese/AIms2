## 5. The "fewer round-1 zero outcomes" sentence

You were right to doubt it, and it was false. I computed these numbers in R earlier in the session:

| | Round-1 delegators | Zero payoff |
|---|---|---|
| Preference-based | 63 | 36.5% |
| EV | 79 | 31.6% |

Preference-based participants had slightly more zero outcomes, not fewer (Fisher p = 0.59).

The weaker significance comes from the smaller number of round-1 delegators (62 vs. 79 with valid round-2 data). The two contrasts also do not differ from each other (treatment-by-payoff logit interaction p = 0.772). I replaced the sentence with that, and added the check to the Rmd. I also corrected the same wording in `RnR/Reviewer2_response.md`.
