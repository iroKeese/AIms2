When personalization deters delegation to algorithms: Evidence from a preregistered experiment

- Personalization deters delegation to algorithms rather than increasing it
- At first contact, delegation is lower for preference-based than EV algorithms
- Categorical non-delegation is far more common under personalization (16% vs 3%)
- Resistance is authority-based, not accuracy-based: users trust the personal rule
- After feedback, the EV algorithm loses more followers: it yields zero more often
